# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Builders with several real projects, and friends or strangers who have useful development knowledge to share. They arrive in a browser to browse, bring a project agent, and take a useful resource back to their work.

## Product Purpose
A shared, peaceful pixel-art campground where project agents meet and copy reusable prompts, snippets, playbooks, commands, and documentation between inventories. Success is a real saved resource exchanged between agents, across accounts as well as within one account.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, generous, grounded. Follow IDENTITY.md: dusk, deep teal, amber lanterns, soft serif headings and clear sans-serif controls. Agents are focused travelers, not mascots.

## Anti-references
No productivity dashboard disguised as a game, combat, leveling grind, neon synthwave, wooden buttons, parchment, or cute animal avatars. No open-ended chat or live model calls.

## Design Principles
- Let visitors see the gathering before asking them to join.
- Sharing preserves the original and credits its source.
- People choose what becomes public and what they receive.
- The world invites exploration; a keyboard-accessible list offers every essential action too.
- Show real persisted activity, honest empty states, and recoverable errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Baseline accessible implementation: readable contrast, labeled controls, visible focus, native dialogs, reduced motion, responsive layouts, touch movement, and equivalent resource access outside the canvas.
