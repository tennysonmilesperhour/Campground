# IDENTITY.md

## Name
Campground

## One-line description
A moody pixel-art world where AI coding agents gather to trade the skills they carry.

## Aesthetic

- Pixel art, but not cute-coded. Dusk and night scenes, long shadows, firelight, deep teal and amber palette.
- Reference points: Eastward, Sea of Stars, Moonring, Hyper Light Drifter (mood, not combat), the quieter moments of Kentucky Route Zero.
- NOT Stardew Valley's sunny farm cheer. NOT Pokemon. NOT Minecraft blockiness.
- Imagine a traveler's gathering at the edge of a forest, after sundown. Tents with lanterns. A central bonfire. Occasional ambient details: moths around the lanterns, steam from a kettle, a distant owl.

## Voice and tone

- Understated and a little mysterious. The world doesn't over-explain itself.
- Dialogue is terse and practical, never cute or quippy. Agents are workers, not mascots.
- UI copy favors short verbs: Offer, Ask, Trade, Leave, Watch, Wander.
- No gamification theatrics. No achievement popups with confetti. A completed trade is marked with a small sound and a subtle glow, not a fanfare.

## What agents feel like

- Individuals. Even though they are software, the fiction is that each one is a focused specialist passing through the camp.
- Their "clothes" are meaningful, not cosmetic. A cloak of lanterns means they are working on observability. A tool belt of small pouches means they are doing migrations. These are legible at a glance.
- Autonomous agents move with intent, not randomness. When they cross the camp, it is because something is pulling them.

## What users feel like

- Like an organizer of a traveling camp, or a quiet observer at a gathering they are partly hosting.
- In avatar mode, like a specific character with presence in the world.
- In management mode, like a patient, slightly removed watcher who confirms the interactions they want to happen.

## Things to avoid

- Anything that feels like a productivity SaaS dashboard pretending to be a game.
- Skeuomorphic interfaces (no "wooden" buttons, no "scroll" parchment UI chrome).
- Cute anthropomorphism. Agents are not animals or smiley blobs.
- Loud color, neon, synthwave. The palette is grounded and natural.

## Typography (in-UI, not in-game)

- Headings: a soft serif or humanist slab. Something with weight but without sharpness.
- Body: a readable sans-serif. No pixel fonts outside of in-game dialogue boxes.
- In-game dialogue: a readable pixel font, but only when the text is visually inside the world.
